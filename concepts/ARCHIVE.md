# The Concept Archive
### Eleven roads not taken — preserved from the bake-off that chose Proving Grounds

The studio developed twelve concepts for an iPhone PWA game with high week-two retention. Five were built as playable demos; **Proving Grounds (Concept 12) won** and is now the full game in this repository. This file preserves the other eleven for future reference — any of them could be game two. Full documents remain in this directory; deep-dives (where written) are in `deep-dives/`.

---

## The demo finalists (playable builds preserved in the demo archive zip)

### 01 — Bring It Down *(demolition physics)*
Controlled-demolition puzzles with honest structural simulation: cut beams, place charges on a detonation timeline, and drop buildings into a chalk footprint without touching the neighbors. Signature tech: a pre-stress solver so intact buildings genuinely carry load and collapse cascades emerge from real force re-routing. Retention spine: star mastery → procedural daily contracts → player-built structures (UGC editor). *Demo: "The Corner Office."* [Full doc](01-bring-it-down.md) · [Deep-dive](deep-dives/01-bring-it-down-deep-dive.md)

### 02 — Trickshot Open *(physics trick-shot golf)*
Committed slingshot flicks with real spin/wind/material physics across absurd urban holes; multiple lines per hole plus one authored "Dream Shot." Signature architecture: fully deterministic ~600-line bespoke ball sim → ghosts, replay anti-cheat, and tappable challenge links all fall out of input recording. Retention spine: daily course + ghost racing + promotion/relegation divisions. *Demo: "Loading Bay," with a rail-skip Dream Shot.* [Full doc](02-trickshot-open.md) · [Deep-dive](deep-dives/02-trickshot-open-deep-dive.md)

### 04 — The Bracelet *(poker career)*
A poker *career*, not a poker app: kitchen games to the Main Event against persistent AI characters with honest, learnable leaks — scouted hand-by-hand in a Notebook that never forgets. Bankroll management is the meta-game; the deck is provably fair. Signature tech: three-layer AI (trained baseline → personality bias → slow adaptation). *Demo: "The Kitchen Game" — 1,178 engine assertions, five believable villains.* [Full doc](04-the-bracelet.md) · [Deep-dive](deep-dives/04-the-bracelet-deep-dive.md)

### 11 — Wildcat *(oil-boom tycoon — the first wild card)*
Sonar the land, thumb-steer the drill around bedrock and gas, sell crude into a breathing market with news spikes, and buy upgrades that visibly change the rig. Rags-to-riches with a survey/drill/sell/upgrade four-beat loop and seeded seasonal leases. *Demo: "Prospect Flats" — economy proven across 300 bot runs.* [Full doc](11-wildcat.md)

## The concept-stage seven

### 03 — From Nothing *(rags-to-riches life sim)*
$40 and a bus ticket in a living procedural city; every run writes a different biography. Consequence-driven event engine (30%+ of events call back to your named past choices), legacy origins, weekly seeded cities, shareable Life Cards. The strongest *story-retention* concept; the honest cost is a permanent writers' room. [Full doc](03-from-nothing.md) · [Deep-dive](deep-dives/03-from-nothing-deep-dive.md)

### 05 — Threshold *(precision skill gauntlets)*
400 hand-crafted 20–40-second one-thumb gauntlets — pure skill, zero RNG, instant retry, hazards moving on the beat. Medal mastery, ghost racing, a brutal daily. Best-in-class retention of its hooked cohort; narrower funnel. [Full doc](05-threshold.md)

### 06 — Dead Load *(engineering construction puzzles)*
Node-and-beam bridge/contraption building stress-tested by real physics; cheapest-working-solution leaderboards and solution galleries. Proven genre endgame via community-built contracts. Overlapped audience with 01 — the team recommended shipping at most one. [Full doc](06-dead-load.md)

### 07 — Ante Up City *(street-trader economy sim)*
Pure trading rags-to-riches across a simulated city economy: buy low, haggle, arbitrage news events, scale from backpack to warehouse empire. Seeded 4-week seasonal ladders; push notifications that are *fictionally native* ("the port just closed"). [Full doc](07-ante-up-city.md)

### 08 — Hexen Draft *(roguelite hex-board engine-builder)*
Draft tiles into a spatial board that IS your character; adjacency triggers cascade, enemies break the board itself, drafting is placement. The genre with the industry's best day-30 curves, given an original core. [Full doc](08-hexen-draft.md)

### 09 — Southpaw *(boxing manager)*
Club fighter to champion from the corner stool: camps, matchmaking, promoters, between-round calls. Careers have acts, rivals, and endings by nature; legacy gym meta across retirements; seasonal shared-prospect drafts. [Full doc](09-southpaw.md)

### 10 — Lockbox *(storage-auction appraisal)*
Thirty seconds of flashlight through a roller door, live auctions against rivals with tells, then dig, appraise, and flip. Knowledge-as-progression: the player genuinely gets better, and the game proves it daily. The sleeper-hit candidate. [Full doc](10-lockbox.md) · [Deep-dive](deep-dives/10-lockbox-deep-dive.md)
